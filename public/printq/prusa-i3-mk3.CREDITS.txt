prusa-i3-mk3.glb: 3D model of the Original Prusa i3 MK3 shown on the PrintQ pages.

Based on "Original Prusa i3 MK3 for Fusion 360 (and STL)" by Swesen
  https://www.printables.com/model/179638  (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
which is based on "Prusa i3 MK3 SolidWorks Assembly (with STEP)" by jzkmath
  https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2699771  https://github.com/jzkmath/Original-Prusa-i3  (GNU GPL)
derived from the Original Prusa i3 by Prusa Research
  https://github.com/prusa3d/Original-Prusa-i3  (GPL-2.0)

Changes by the UFV CSA: decimated from 1.67M to 150k triangles with meshoptimizer,
